01 / About

A little about me.

My experience in higher education student support has given me a practical understanding of the learner experience, from navigating online learning environments to developing academic skills and engaging with educational content.

With a BA Honours in Psychology and a Postgraduate Diploma in Public Health, I bring an interdisciplinary perspective to learning design, combining an understanding of human behaviour, learning and the broader contexts in which people access information.

I am building on this experience through instructional design, digital learning development and educational technology, with a particular interest in how technology can make learning more accessible, relevant and effective.
